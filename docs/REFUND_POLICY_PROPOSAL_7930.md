# CRYPTO LAB v79 — Refund Policy Proposal v1

Status: decision required. This proposal is not published, does not authorize refunds, and does not activate payments.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to paid monthly access to CRYPTO LAB digital services. It preserves all mandatory consumer rights under applicable law.

## 2. Refund eligibility

A full or proportional refund may be approved when:

1. the same payment was charged or credited more than once;
2. a verified payment did not activate the purchased plan within the stated or reasonable service window;
3. the paid digital service was not provided;
4. the service had a material non-conformity that was not remedied within a reasonable time;
5. a material service modification negatively affected access or use and applicable law grants a termination or refund right;
6. an unauthorized payment is confirmed after ownership and transaction review;
7. a refund is otherwise required by applicable law.

## 3. Cancellation

Cancellation stops future renewal. It does not automatically create a refund for a correctly delivered and conforming period, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Recurring billing remains disabled until separately approved.

## 4. Cases not ordinarily eligible

Except where applicable law requires otherwise, refunds are not granted solely because of:

- trading losses, missed profit, market movement, or reliance on AI-generated analysis;
- dissatisfaction with a market outcome when the service itself was delivered as described;
- sending unsupported assets, using the wrong network, or sending to an address not shown in the verified invoice;
- blockchain network fees or delays outside CRYPTO LAB control;
- violation of the Terms of Use or abuse of the service.

These exclusions never limit mandatory rights relating to non-delivery, material non-conformity, duplicate payment, or other legally protected claims.

## 5. Request and evidence

A request must be submitted through the in-app support channel or the published operator email and include:

- account email or account identifier;
- invoice identifier;
- transaction hash;
- payment network and asset;
- reason for the request and supporting evidence.

CRYPTO LAB may request reasonable proof of control of the paying or return wallet. Seed phrases, private keys, and wallet passwords must never be requested or provided.

## 6. Review and timing

Requests are reviewed manually. Approved refunds or proportional price reductions are completed within a reasonable time and no later than 14 calendar days after a valid request or termination notice when that deadline is required by applicable law.

## 7. Refund method

Where technically and legally possible, the refund is returned in the same stablecoin and on the same network used for the payment, to a verified return address. If that is impossible, the parties may agree on an equivalent method. A mandatory refund must not impose a refund-processing or network fee on the consumer.

## 8. Access after refund

A full refund ends the corresponding paid access. A proportional refund may shorten or adjust the paid period. User-created exportable data remains available in accordance with the Privacy Notice and applicable law.

## 9. Fraud and sanctions review

CRYPTO LAB may pause a refund only for the time reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, duplicate claims, sanctions, or wallet-ownership concerns, without limiting mandatory legal deadlines.

## 10. Activation boundary

This proposal does not enable checkout, recurring billing, refunds, receiving addresses, or real transfers. Separate owner approval and technical activation are required.
